## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Real-time AI subtitles" icon="document">
    Speech recognition (Whisper) turns the audio into text, a translation model of your choice turns it into your language, and the result appears as a normal subtitle track, ahead of playback.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Files, discs and the web" icon="laptop">
    Local video files, Blu-ray and DVD, YouTube and videos on ordinary web pages. Subtitles work the same everywhere.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subtitle files without playing" icon="pencil">
    Generate SRT files for a whole video from speech, from a text subtitle track or from disc image subtitles (OCR), with one translated file per language.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Glossary and style" icon="open-book">
    Character and place names stay consistent. Review and edit the glossary, choose polite, casual or natural speech, and existing subtitles are re-translated.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sharper pictures" icon="star">
    GPU upscaling (high-quality scaling, NVIDIA RTX Video Super Resolution, your own shaders) and built-in picture-quality presets for anime and compressed video.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private by default" icon="approve-check">
    No playback history, logs or subtitle caches are kept unless you ask for them. Web and YouTube windows always run in InPrivate mode. No telemetry.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Good to know

- **Windows only.** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with a CPU that supports AVX2. An NVIDIA GPU is optional and speeds up speech recognition.
- **You need a translation model.** Subtitles are translated by an Ollama-compatible server, local or cloud. See [Getting started](getting-started/#translation-server).
- **Disc decryption is a separate, optional download.** Unprotected discs, folders and ISO images play with the normal installer. See [Optional Blu-ray/DVD decryption components](disc-decryption/).
- **Open source.** NanumPlayer is a modified mpv (GPL-2.0-or-later) with whisper.cpp (MIT). See [License and notices](license/).
