Choose a method under **Upscale** in the **Picture** button's menu (or **Ctrl+I**, or **Ctrl+N → Picture quality**). The menu stays open so you can see each change, and the button shows a blue dot while an upscale method or picture correction is on. It changes while the video plays and is saved in `script-opts\nanum-upscale.conf` in your settings folder. This is independent of CUDA speech recognition: you do **not** need the CUDA Toolkit for upscaling.

Under Upscale it lists Default, High-quality scaling, RTX Automatic, RTX 1.5x, 2x and 3x, and User shaders." caption="The Picture button on the playback bar opens the menu; the Upscale choices are at the top." widths={[480, 800]} />

| Method | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Default** | Turns off the extra processing and restores the renderer and scaler settings this feature changed. Your own filters and shaders are kept. |
| **High-quality scaling** | On a GPU renderer, uses `ewa_lanczossharp` with ringing suppression 0.6. A better scaler, not an AI restoration. |
| **NVIDIA RTX upscaling** | Uses `gpu-next`, Direct3D 11, D3D11VA and the NVIDIA extension of the `d3d11vpp` filter. Needs a supported NVIDIA GPU and driver, and RTX Video Super Resolution switched on in the NVIDIA app or control panel. |
| **User shaders** | Add mpv-compatible `.glsl`, `.hook` or `.frag` files one at a time. Select an entry to reorder or remove it. They run after existing shaders, in the order you chose. |

No third-party shader packs (such as Anime4K or FSRCNNX) are bundled; you can add them yourself.

## RTX scale

Choose **Auto, 1.5x, 2x or 3x**.

- **Auto** computes from the source and window size, rounds up in 0.25 steps, caps at 4x, and removes the filter when no enlargement is needed. Window resizes are applied after a short pause.
- **Fixed factors** scale both width and height, for example 2x takes 1080p to 4K. Do not blindly apply a fixed factor to a video that is already 4K or a window that is smaller than the source.

<Aside type="note">
  The status "Requested" means the filter was requested. It does not confirm that the driver is doing AI processing internally. RTX HDR conversion is never enabled, and HDR or 10-bit support depends on your driver and the video path.
</Aside>

## Good to know

- The renderer can briefly recreate the picture when you switch methods.
- An unsupported GPU or a shader compile error appears in the status, and the processing this feature added is removed.
- Speech recognition, a local translation model and upscaling all use the GPU; together they can drop frames. Lower the factor or try high-quality scaling.
